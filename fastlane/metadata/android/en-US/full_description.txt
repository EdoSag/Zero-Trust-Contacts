Most apps on your phone can read your address book. Zero-Trust Contacts keeps your contacts in an end-to-end encrypted vault instead, and you choose which ones, if any, the rest of the phone gets to see.

WHAT IT DOES
• A proper editor: several phones, emails and addresses with Mobile, Work and Home labels, birthdays, notes and photos.
• Labels (groups): create, rename, and email or text a whole label.
• Quick actions: call, text, email, Signal, WhatsApp and directions; share as vCard or QR code.
• Optional caller ID: show chosen contacts in the phone's dialer and messaging apps.
• Scan a business card with on-device text recognition (English and Hebrew).
• Duplicate warnings and a merge tool, plus multi-select to label, share or delete.
• A to Z fast scroll, sorting by first or last name, and correct Hebrew and English ordering.
• Trash that keeps deleted contacts for 30 days, and undo for changes.
• Import from Google, the phone's accounts, the SIM card, iCloud or Outlook vCards and CSV files.
• Open .vcf files and contact QR codes, export to vCard, and encrypted backups.
• Home-screen widget for your favourite people.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
